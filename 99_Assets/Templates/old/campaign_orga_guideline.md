# Obsidian Campaign Organization Guideline (Cosmic Horror Style)

**Core Philosophy:** Structure your campaign vault as a dynamic web of interconnected information, focusing on the *situation* rather than a fixed plot. Prioritize flexibility, track consequences, and leverage Obsidian's linking capabilities for clarity during planning and updating.

## I. Vault Setup & Structure

*   **[ ] Recommended Folder Structure:** Implement a clear, shallow folder structure.
    *   `00 - CAMPAIGN HUB`: Central planning, dashboards, trackers.
    *   `01 - ADVENTURES`: Subfolders for each adventure (using your adventure template).
    *   `02 - NPCs`: Notes for significant recurring NPCs.
    *   `03 - LOCATIONS`: Notes for significant recurring locations.
    *   `04 - FACTIONS & THREATS`: Notes for cults, entities, organizations.
    *   `05 - LORE & MYTHOS`: Notes for key tomes, concepts, spells.
    *   `06 - PLAYER CHARACTERS`: Tracking notes for Investigators.
*   **[ ] Consistent Naming Conventions:** Use clear and consistent names for notes (e.g., `[[NPC Name]]`, `[[Adventure Title]]`, `[[Faction Name]]`).
*   **[ ] Utilize Templates:** Create simple templates not just for adventures, but also for NPCs, Factions, Locations, and Lore entries to ensure consistent information structure.

## II. Central Campaign Hub (`00 - CAMPAIGN HUB`)

*   **[ ] `[[Campaign Dashboard]]` Note:** Create a central hub note.
    *   **Contents:** Pitch, Themes, Central Mystery, Links to Main Antagonists/Factions, Key NPCs/Locations, Potential Arcs (flexible!), Current Status, Open Threads, Links to Timelines/Flowcharts.
    *   **Purpose:** Provides a high-level overview and entry point to all major campaign elements. Keep it updated.
*   **[ ] `[[Campaign Timeline / Countdown Clock]]` Note:** Track significant past events and future antagonist plans/deadlines.
    *   **Method:** Use dates or relative time markers. Focus on "what happens if the Investigators *don't* intervene."
    *   **Purpose:** Drives urgency and simulates a living world independent of player action. Reflects situation-based design.
*   **[ ] `[[Campaign Consequences Log]]` Note:** Maintain a running log of major event outcomes and their impact on the campaign world.
    *   **Format:** Dated entries summarizing key results of adventures or major player actions. Link heavily to affected notes ([[Adventure X]], [[NPC Y]], [[Faction Z]]).
    *   **Purpose:** Tracks the evolving state of the campaign and provides context for future events.
*   **[ ] `[[Campaign Flowchart / Connections]]` (Optional - Use Canvas/Mind Map):** Visualize high-level connections.
    *   **Focus:** Map dependencies between adventures based on **Blocker Clues**. Show *intended* paths, not every possibility.
    *   **Granularity:** Keep it high-level (Adventure A leads to B or C). Don't map internal adventure structure here.
    *   **Purpose:** Planning tool to visualize prerequisites and potential campaign flow; *not* a railroad map.

## III. Core Element Notes (NPCs, Factions, Lore, etc.)

*   **[ ] Dedicated Notes per Element:** Each significant recurring NPC, Location, Faction, Threat, Tome, or Mythos concept gets its own note in the appropriate folder.
*   **[ ] Faction/Threat Notes - Situation Focus:** For cults, entities, organizations:
    *   Detail their **Goals** (short & long term).
    *   Outline their **Plan/Phases** (how they intend to achieve goals).
    *   List their **Resources** (members, assets, knowledge, powers).
    *   Describe their likely **Reactions** to specific setbacks or successes (by Investigators).
    *   Link to key member [[NPC Notes]] and related [[Lore Notes]].
    *   **Purpose:** Defines the active opposition as a dynamic entity, crucial for situation-based play.
*   **[ ] NPC Notes:** Include motivations, knowledge (what clues do they hold?), relationships (links!), stats, and potential reactions.
*   **[ ] Lore Notes:** Explain Mythos concepts, tome contents, spell effects. Link to where this lore can be discovered ([[Adventure X]], [[NPC Y]]).
*   **[ ] Heavy Linking:** Use `[[Wikilinks]]` extensively within *all* notes to connect related concepts (e.g., an NPC note links to the faction they belong to, the adventures they appear in, and the lore they know).

## IV. Player Character Tracking (`06 - PLAYER CHARACTERS`)

*   **[ ] Individual PC Notes:** Maintain a separate note for each Investigator.
*   **[ ] Track Key Stats:** Record changes in SAN, HP, MP, Luck.
*   **[ ] Track Cumulative Effects:** Log acquired phobias/manias, significant injuries, increases in Cthulhu Mythos skill, learned spells.
*   **[ ] Track Relationships/Bonds:** Note changes in relationships with key NPCs or impact on defined Bonds (if using that optional rule).
*   **[ ] Track Important Knowledge/Items:** List significant clues, artifacts, or incriminating evidence they possess.
*   **Purpose:** Provides a clear record of the toll the campaign takes on the Investigators, informing roleplaying and future consequences.

## V. Maintaining Flexibility & Flow

*   **[ ] Post-Adventure Updates:** After each adventure session:
    *   Update the `[[Campaign Consequences Log]]`.
    *   Update the `[[Campaign Timeline / Countdown Clock]]` (did the antagonist's plan change?).
    *   Update relevant `[[Faction/Threat Notes]]` (how do they react?).
    *   Update affected `[[NPC Notes]]` and `[[Location Notes]]`.
    *   Update `[[PC Notes]]` with SAN changes, knowledge gained, etc.
    *   Update the "Current Status" and "Open Threads" on the `[[Campaign Dashboard]]`.
*   **[ ] Embrace Emergent Leads:** If players create a new, unexpected path:
    *   Create new notes (NPCs, locations, clues) as needed.
    *   Link them into the existing web.
    *   Update Faction/Threat notes to reflect how antagonists might react to this unexpected development. *Do not force players back onto a predetermined path.*
*   **[ ] Use Tags for Status:** Employ tags to mark the current state of elements (e.g., `#status/active_threat`, `#status/defeated`, `#npc/missing`, `#lead/active`, `#lead/cold`). This helps filter information on your dashboard or via search.

## VI. Using Obsidian Features Effectively

*   **[ ] Leverage Linking:** This is paramount. Link everything that relates.
*   **[ ] Use Tags:** Classify notes (`#npc`, `#location`, `#faction`) and track status (`#status/active`).
*   **[ ] Backlinks Pane:** Regularly check backlinks to see what connects *to* a specific note – great for understanding context.
*   **[ ] Graph View (Use Sparingly):** Can be overwhelming but occasionally useful for visualizing clusters of connected information.
*   **[ ] Search:** Use Obsidian's powerful search to find specific details quickly during planning.
*   **[ ] Canvas/Mind Maps:** Use for visual tools like flowcharts, relationship maps, or timelines. Embed these in or link from your Hub notes.
