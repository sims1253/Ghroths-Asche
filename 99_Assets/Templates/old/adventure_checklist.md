# Investigation Adventure Design Checklist

## I. Core Concept & Hook
- [ ] **Clear Premise:** Is the central mystery/problem clear and intriguing?
- [ ] **Compelling Hook:** Why are the *player characters* involved? Is the initial motivation strong enough?
- [ ] **Stakes:** What's at risk (immediately and potentially long-term)? Are the stakes clear (or become clear) to the players?

## II. Investigation Structure & Clues
- [ ] **Node-Based Design (Recommended):** Is the adventure structured around locations/NPCs/events (nodes) rather than a strict linear sequence?
- [ ] **Blocker Clues Identified:** Have you explicitly identified the *essential* clues needed to progress the core plot?
- [ ] **The Three Clue Rule:** For each Blocker conclusion, are there *at least three distinct ways* (clues/methods) for players to reach it?
- [ ] **Tiered Clue Structure (Blocker, Background, Vibes):**
    - [ ] **Blockers:** Clearly defined and discoverable (see above).
    - [ ] **Relevant Background:** Are there clues offering context, motive, lore, weaknesses, or alternative paths? Are these rewarding for deeper investigation?
    - [ ] **Vibes:** Are there clues primarily for atmosphere, mood, and foreshadowing integrated into scenes?
- [ ] **Clue Delivery:**
    - [ ] **Variety:** Are clues presented through diverse means (dialogue, documents, forensics, environment, psychic flashes, etc.)?
    - [ ] **Show, Don't Tell:** Is information revealed through discovery and interaction rather than GM exposition?
    - [ ] **Handouts:** Are there well-designed handouts for key textual/visual clues?
    - [ ] **Avoid Info Dumps:** Is information broken down into digestible chunks?
- [ ] **Interpretation Focus:** Is the primary challenge interpreting clues and deciding action, rather than just finding them?
- [ ] **Avoid Bottlenecks:** Have potential single points of failure been identified and mitigated (e.g., via the Three Clue Rule)?
- [ ] **Avoid Pixel Bitching:** Are core clues reasonably discoverable without requiring players to guess hyper-specific actions?

## III. Locations & NPCs
- [ ] **Evocative Locations:** Are locations described with sensory details that enhance atmosphere and provide potential clues (Vibes/Background)?
- [ ] **Interactive Environments:** Do locations offer meaningful things for players to interact with?
- [ ] **NPC Roles & Knowledge:** Do key NPCs have clear roles, motivations, and defined knowledge (what Blockers/Background they possess)?
- [ ] **NPC Reactivity:** How might NPCs react to different player approaches (cooperation, threats, suspicion)?
- [ ] **Antagonist/Threat:**
    - [ ] **Clear Goal/Plan:** Does the primary threat have a understandable (if alien) objective and plan?
    - [ ] **Timeline/Escalation:** Does the threat operate on a timeline or escalate if players delay or fail? (Consider a "Countdown Clock").
    - [ ] **Reactivity:** How does the threat react if its plans are disrupted?

## IV. Pacing, Atmosphere & Climax
- [ ] **Pacing Variety:** Does the adventure mix investigation scenes with potential tension, action, social interaction, or horror moments?
- [ ] **Atmosphere & Tone:** Is the desired mood (dread, mystery, cosmic horror) consistently maintained through description and events?
- [ ] **Horror Elements:** Are the horror elements integrated effectively (psychological, body horror, cosmic dread, sanity effects)?
- [ ] **Meaningful Climax:** Does the adventure build towards a significant confrontation or resolution? Is it more than just a combat encounter?
- [ ] **Multiple Outcomes:** Are different degrees of success or failure possible based on player actions and discoveries?
- [ ] **Loose Ends (Optional):** Does the adventure intentionally leave hooks or unresolved elements for future campaign play?

## V. Player Agency & Character Integration
- [ ] **Meaningful Choices:** Do players have opportunities to make choices that genuinely affect the investigation's direction and outcome?
- [ ] **Character Relevance:**
    - [ ] Are there opportunities for different character skills/backgrounds to shine?
    - [ ] Are there ties (potential or actual) to character backstories, motivations, Bonds, etc.?
    - [ ] Are the potential consequences (physical, mental, social) personally relevant?
- [ ] **Fail Forward:** If players fail a roll or miss a clue, does it lead to complications or partial success rather than a dead stop?

## VI. GM Support
- [ ] **Clear Presentation:** Is the adventure layout clear for the GM, separating descriptive text, clue details, NPC stats, etc.?
- [ ] **Contingency Notes:** Are there brief notes on handling common player deviations (e.g., killing a key NPC early)?
- [ ] **System Integration:** Are necessary game system mechanics (skill checks, sanity rules, combat stats) clearly referenced?
