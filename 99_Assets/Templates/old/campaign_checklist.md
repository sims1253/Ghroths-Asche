# Investigation Campaign Design Checklist

## I. Core Concept & Theme
- [ ] **Central Theme:** Is there a clear, unifying theme (e.g., decay, forbidden knowledge, cosmic indifference, loss of humanity)?
- [ ] **Overarching Mystery/Conspiracy:** Is there a larger puzzle, threat, or organization that connects the individual adventures?
- [ ] **Campaign Scope:** What is the intended length and scale of the campaign?
- [ ] **Tone Consistency:** Is the desired tone sustainable and reinforced across the campaign?

## II. Campaign Structure & Arcs
- [ ] **Major Arcs:** Is the campaign broken down into logical arcs or phases with escalating stakes?
- [ ] **Connecting Adventures:**
    - [ ] **Clear Links:** How do individual adventures connect to the main plot or themes? (Recurring NPCs, related cults, building lore, consequences).
    - [ ] **Variety:** Do the adventures offer variety in style, location, and type of threat while still feeling connected?
- [ ] **Pacing (Macro):** Is there consideration for downtime, shifts in intensity, and breathing room between major arcs?
- [ ] **Introduction:** Does the first adventure (or session 0) effectively introduce the campaign's core elements and tone?
- [ ] **Mid-Campaign Turning Points:** Are there planned significant revelations or shifts in the status quo?

## III. Antagonists & World
- [ ] **Primary Antagonist(s)/Force:** Is the main source of opposition clearly defined (even if mysterious initially)?
    - [ ] **Long-Term Goals:** What is their ultimate objective over the campaign's span?
    - [ ] **Resources & Methods:** What tools, followers, or powers do they wield? How do they operate?
    - [ ] **Evolution/Reaction:** Does the antagonist force react and adapt to player successes and failures over time?
- [ ] **Setting & Worldbuilding:**
    - [ ] **Consistent Lore:** Is the hidden lore of the world revealed gradually and consistently?
    - [ ] **Living World:** Does the setting feel alive and react to events (both PC actions and antagonist actions)?
    - [ ] **Recurring Elements:** Are there recurring NPCs, locations, or organizations that evolve over the campaign?

## IV. Character Arcs & Consequences
- [ ] **Character Development:** Are there opportunities for characters to change, learn, and develop (or degrade) over the long term?
- [ ] **Integration of Backstories:** Are player character backstories woven into the campaign plot or side plots?
- [ ] **Long-Term Consequences:**
    - [ ] **Tracking:** Is there a system (formal or informal) for tracking cumulative effects (Sanity loss, Trauma, broken Bonds, acquired knowledge, reputation changes, injuries)?
    - [ ] **Impact:** Do these long-term consequences meaningfully affect gameplay and character choices?
- [ ] **Personal Stakes:** Does the overarching plot eventually create significant personal stakes for the characters?

## V. Information Management & Climax
- [ ] **Information Flow (Macro):** How is crucial information about the central mystery seeded and revealed across multiple adventures?
- [ ] **Foreshadowing:** Are later threats, revelations, or antagonists subtly hinted at earlier in the campaign?
- [ ] **Campaign Climax:**
    - [ ] **Build-Up:** Does the campaign build towards a satisfying (though not necessarily 'happy' or 'victorious' in cosmic horror) conclusion?
    - [ ] **Resolution:** Does the climax address the primary antagonists and overarching mysteries?
    - [ ] **Player Impact:** Do the players' cumulative actions and choices significantly influence the nature of the climax and its outcome?
- [ ] **Epilogue/Aftermath:** Is there consideration for what happens *after* the climax, acknowledging the characters' journeys and the state of the world?

## VI. Flexibility & GM Support
- [ ] **Adaptability:** Is the campaign structure flexible enough to accommodate unexpected player choices and successes/failures?
- [ ] **GM Resources:** Are there tools provided for the GM to manage the campaign's moving parts (timelines, relationship maps, lore summaries)?
- [ ] **System Integration:** How do the chosen RPG system's mechanics support the campaign's themes and long-term play (e.g., advancement, long-term mental health)?
