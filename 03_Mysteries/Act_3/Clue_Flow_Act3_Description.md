# Phase 3 Clue Flow Description (Guide for Canvas)

This document outlines potential clue connections *within* and *between* the core Phase 3 scenarios ([[08 - Death Spiral]], [[09 - Shell]], [[10 - Passage]], [[11 - Burden]]), leading towards the finale ([[12 - Träger der Asche]]). Use this as a guide for your Obsidian Canvas.

**Core Principle:** Players enter Phase 3 knowing the goal ([[Der Fokuspunkt]]), the required action ([[Das Abschirmungsritual]]), and the need for [[Die Komponente]]. The adventures A08-A11 represent the necessary steps to prepare for the finale. While the order might be flexible based on leads, information or resources gained in one often facilitate another.

**Scenario 08: [[08 - Death Spiral]] (Focus: Survival/Resources)**
*   **Primary Clues Found:** Vital resources (fuel, ammo, medical supplies), tactical info on local factions (Kult, Banden), safe routes/shelters.
*   **Potential Cross-Leads:**
    *   **-> A09 (Shell):** Rumors or captured Kult/Banden members might mention a specific location associated with strange energy or a powerful object (leading to [[Ort der Komponente]]). May find maps/notes pointing towards it.
    *   **-> A10 (Passage):** Information about particularly dangerous zones or potential safe routes towards the likely region of [[Der Fokuspunkt]]. May find necessary transport or parts.
    *   **-> A11 (Burden):** Zufallsfund von Widerstands-Notizen oder Kult-Texten mit Ritualfragmenten. Gerüchte über letzte Verstecke des Widerstands.
    *   **-> A12 (Finale):** Success/failure directly impacts starting resources/condition. Weakening local Kult/Banden reduces potential interference later.

**Scenario 09: [[09 - Shell]] (Focus: Komponente Finden)**
*   **Primary Clues Found:** [[Die Komponente]] itself, understanding its nature and the price/Wächter ([[Die Shell Entität]]).
*   **Potential Cross-Leads:**
    *   **-> A08 (Death Spiral):** The nature of the Komponente or the Wächter might require specific resources only obtainable through scavenging/trading in the ruins.
    *   **-> A10 (Passage):** The location of the Komponente might reveal clues about the best route to the Fokuspunkt, or the Wächter might provide cryptic directions/warnings.
    *   **-> A11 (Burden):** Understanding the Komponente's nature is crucial for completing the Ritualwissen. The price paid might heavily impact the group's mental state (Burden).
    *   **-> A12 (Finale):** Securing (or failing to secure) the Komponente is a primary factor for the ritual's success/difficulty. The price paid impacts starting SAN/HP/Moral.

**Scenario 10: [[10 - Passage]] (Focus: Weg zum Fokuspunkt)**
*   **Primary Clues Found:** A viable route to [[Der Fokuspunkt]], understanding the dangers and sacrifices required for the journey.
*   **Potential Cross-Leads:**
    *   **-> A08 (Death Spiral):** Requires resources gathered previously. Encounters on the way might yield last-minute resources or information.
    *   **-> A09 (Shell):** The journey might be necessary *before* finding the Komponente if its location is near the Fokuspunkt, or the secured Komponente might influence the Passage (attracting danger?).
    *   **-> A11 (Burden):** The hardships and sacrifices of the journey directly contribute to the "Burden". May uncover final clues about the Fokuspunkt's state or last Widerstands-messages.
    *   **-> A12 (Finale):** Success/failure determines *if* and *in what condition* the group reaches the location for the finale.

**Scenario 11: [[11 - Burden]] (Focus: Finale Vorbereitung, Ritualwissen)**
*   **Primary Clues Found:** Final, complete understanding of [[Das Abschirmungsritual]], assessment of the [[Der Fokuspunkt]]'s state, mental preparation/confrontation.
*   **Potential Cross-Leads:**
    *   **-> A08 (Death Spiral):** May require a final resource run based on ritual needs discovered here.
    *   **-> A09 (Shell):** Final understanding of how [[Die Komponente]] integrates into the ritual. Confronting the "Burden" might involve reflecting on the price paid for it.
    *   **-> A10 (Passage):** The state of the Fokuspunkt discovered here might depend on how well the Passage was navigated (e.g., did the Kult arrive first?).
    *   **-> A12 (Finale):** Directly sets the stage. Completeness of ritual knowledge and mental state of the group are critical starting conditions.

**Towards the Finale (A12):**
*   Successfully completing (or surviving) A08, A09, A10, and A11 provides the necessary **Resources**, the **Komponente**, the **Route/Access**, and the **Knowledge/Willpower** for the final ritual.
*   Failures in any of these significantly increase the difficulty of [[12 - Träger der Asche]] or alter the potential outcomes.

**Keeper Notes:**
*   Emphasize the interconnectedness. Players might need to backtrack or change plans based on findings (e.g., realizing they need a specific resource from A08 after learning about the Komponente in A09).
*   Use the [[Uhrensystem]] state (set after A07) to modify the difficulty and available leads within each Phase 3 adventure.
*   Track the consequences of each Phase 3 adventure carefully to determine the precise starting conditions for A12.
