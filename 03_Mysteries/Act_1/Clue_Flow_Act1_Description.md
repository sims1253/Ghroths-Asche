# Act 1 Clue Flow Description (Guide for Canvas)

This document outlines the intended flow of clues and information across the three investigations of Act 1, designed to seed the different investigation paths (Ziele) for Phase 2. Use this as a guide to build your visual flowchart in Obsidian Canvas.

**Core Principle:** The goal of Act 1 is to introduce the core mystery ([[Die Asche]]) and provide multiple, distinct leads pointing towards the different facets of the problem explored in Phase 2 (Source, Manifestation, Solution, Cult). The Rule of Three should be applied where possible, especially for critical leads like contacting [[Dr. Aris Thorne]].

## Investigation 1: [[01 - Der Asche-Zorn von Black Creek]]

*   **Hook:** Brutal murders/disappearances in [[Black Creek]].
*   **Key Discovery Location:** [[Tatorte Black Creek]], [[Versteck Täter Black Creek]], [[Sheriff-Posten Black Creek]], (Optional) [[Lokales Archiv/Zeitungsbüro Black Creek]].
*   **Key NPCs:** [[Sheriff Brody]], [[Täter Black Creek]], [[Stadtbewohner Black Creek]].

*   **Clues Found & Leads Generated:**
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 2 (Manifestation):**
        *   **Clue:** [[Die Asche]] Staub found at crime scenes.
        *   **Discovery:** Analysis reveals unknown elements & **anomalous energy signatures**. (Requires scientific analysis skill/contact).
        *   **Path:** Found at [[Tatorte Black Creek]].
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 2 (Manifestation):**
        *   **Clue:** Strange, possibly **geometric patterns** at crime scenes or in killer's lair.
        *   **Discovery:** Unconscious/ritualistic markings suggesting spatial anomalies or points of power.
        *   **Path:** Found at [[Tatorte Black Creek]], [[Versteck Täter Black Creek]] (Kritzeleien).
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 1 (Quelle):**
        *   **Clue:** Killer's obsessive **astronomical symbols** (incorrectly drawn).
        *   **Discovery:** Suggests an external, possibly cosmic influence or source.
        *   **Path:** Found in [[Versteck Täter Black Creek]] (Kritzeleien).
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 1 (Quelle):**
        *   **Clue:** Reports of strange **noises** ("Gesang", "Druck", Dröhnen) or **technological disturbances** (Funkstörungen).
        *   **Discovery:** Hints at an external signal or frequency affecting the area/people.
        *   **Path:** Mentioned by [[Täter Black Creek]] (if captured), [[Stadtbewohner Black Creek]], [[Sheriff Brody]]?
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 3 (Lösung):**
        *   **Clue:** (Optional) Local legends/archives mention **cycles of "bad times"** or **old "safe places"/rituals**.
        *   **Discovery:** Hints at historical context and potential countermeasures.
        *   **Path:** Found in [[Lokales Archiv/Zeitungsbüro Black Creek]].
    *   **Lead -> Contact [[Dr. Aris Thorne]]:**
        *   **Clue:** Researching the **anomalous energy signatures** found in [[Die Asche]].
        *   **Discovery:** Could lead to academic papers or mentions of [[Dr. Aris Thorne]]'s fringe work. (Requires Library Use/Academic contacts).
        *   **Path:** Follow-up research after analyzing Ash from [[Tatorte Black Creek]].
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 4 (Kult):**
        *   **Clue:** (Optional) Unconscious scribbles might resemble an early form of the [[Asche-Chor Symbol (Früh)]].
        *   **Discovery:** Vague hint of a symbol associated with the phenomenon.
        *   **Path:** Found in [[Versteck Täter Black Creek]] (Kritzeleien).

## Investigation 2: [[02 - Das Gewicht, das verweilt]]

*   **Hook:** Investigating a "haunted" or psychically contaminated location ([[Kontaminierter Ort]]).
*   **Key Discovery Location:** [[Kontaminierter Ort]], [[Lokale Bibliothek/Archiv]], (Optional) [[Universitätsarchive/Online-Recherche]].
*   **Key NPCs:** [[Traumatisierte Überlebende]], Psychische Echos, [[Dr. Aris Thorne]] (Potential Contact).

*   **Clues Found & Leads Generated:**
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 3 (Lösung):**
        *   **Clue:** Local legends/archives mention **cycles of similar events** or **past attempts at cleansing/warding**.
        *   **Discovery:** Reinforces historical context and potential solutions.
        *   **Path:** Found in [[Lokale Bibliothek/Archiv]]. (Rule of Three: Similar info potentially in A1 archive).
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 1 (Quelle) / Ziel 2 (Manifestation):**
        *   **Clue:** Persistent **technological disturbances** (EMF spikes, radio static, device malfunction).
        *   **Discovery:** Suggests an ongoing energy source or signal affecting the area.
        *   **Path:** Experienced/Measured at [[Kontaminierter Ort]]. (Rule of Three: Similar info potentially from A1 reports).
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 2 (Manifestation):**
        *   **Clue:** **Architectural anomalies** or spatial distortions reported/witnessed.
        *   **Discovery:** Hints that the phenomenon affects physical space.
        *   **Path:** Observed at [[Kontaminierter Ort]], mentioned by [[Traumatisierte Überlebende]].
    *   **Lead -> Contact [[Dr. Aris Thorne]]:**
        *   **Clue:** Direct research into similar psychic phenomena or anomalous energy fields.
        *   **Discovery:** Leads to [[Dr. Aris Thorne]]'s controversial work.
        *   **Path:** [[Universitätsarchive/Online-Recherche]]. (Rule of Three: Alternative path to finding Thorne).
    *   **Lead -> Contact [[Dr. Aris Thorne]]:**
        *   **Clue:** Referral from local expert or survivor familiar with fringe theories.
        *   **Discovery:** Points investigators towards Thorne as someone who might understand.
        *   **Path:** Interaction with [[Traumatisierte Überlebende]] or other local contact. (Rule of Three: Third potential path to Thorne).
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 4 (Kult):**
        *   **Clue:** (Optional) Survivors mention **organized groups exploiting the situation** or performing strange rituals.
        *   **Discovery:** First hint of a human element capitalizing on the horror.
        *   **Path:** Testimony from [[Traumatisierte Überlebende]].

## Investigation 3: [[03 - Der Schattengefaltete Chor]]

*   **Hook:** Investigating disappearances near specific locations, reports of "empty" people, or lead from [[Dr. Aris Thorne]].
*   **Key Discovery Location:** [[Ort der Transformation]], [[Versteckter Kultisten-Schrein]], [[Wohnungen der Vermissten]].
*   **Key NPCs:** [[Die Transformierten]], [[Der Asche-Chor]] Kultisten, [[Potenzielle Rekruten/Opfer]], [[Kurator Müller]] / [[Höhlenforscher Grau]].

*   **Clues Found & Leads Generated:**
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 4 (Kult):**
        *   **Clue:** Direct confrontation/discovery of [[Der Asche-Chor]] Kultists and their [[Asche-Chor Symbol (Früh)]].
        *   **Discovery:** Confirms an organized human faction is involved.
        *   **Path:** Encounter at [[Ort der Transformation]], [[Versteckter Kultisten-Schrein]].
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 1 (Quelle):**
        *   **Clue:** Fragmentary cult texts mentioning a "Singer", "Silence", or "Star".
        *   **Discovery:** Points towards a singular, possibly cosmic, source.
        *   **Path:** Found in [[Versteckter Kultisten-Schrein]].
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 2 (Manifestation):**
        *   **Clue:** Fragmentary cult texts mentioning "Places of Power", "Sacred Geometry", or "Lines of Ash".
        *   **Discovery:** Suggests the phenomenon interacts with specific locations or patterns.
        *   **Path:** Found in [[Versteckter Kultisten-Schrein]]. (Rule of Three: Reinforces geometric clues from A1/A2).
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 2 (Manifestation):**
        *   **Clue:** Transformation process linked to specific resonant location or artifact.
        *   **Discovery:** Shows a physical focus point is needed for intense manifestation.
        *   **Path:** Investigating [[Ort der Transformation]].
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 3 (Lösung):**
        *   **Clue:** Fragmentary cult texts mentioning "old guardians" or those who "resist the cleansing".
        *   **Discovery:** Hints at an opposing force or historical resistance/solutions.
        *   **Path:** Found in [[Versteckter Kultisten-Schrein]]. (Rule of Three: Reinforces historical clues from A1/A2).
    *   **Lead -> Ziel 4 (Kult):**
        *   **Clue:** Cult texts outlining transformation rituals or plans to "strengthen the presence".
        *   **Discovery:** Provides insight into cult methods and goals.
        *   **Path:** Found in [[Versteckter Kultisten-Schrein]].

**Outcome of Act 1:** By the end of Investigation 3, the investigators should have encountered [[Die Asche]], witnessed its effects (violence, psychological haunting, transformation), confirmed the existence of the [[Der Asche-Chor]], potentially contacted [[Dr. Aris Thorne]], and gathered multiple fragmented clues pointing towards all four Phase 2 Goals (Source, Manifestation, Solution, Cult). The activation of the [[Uhrensystem]] signals the transition to the more open, consequence-driven Phase 2.
