# Phase 2 Clue Flow Description (Guide for Canvas)

This document outlines potential clue connections *within* and *between* the core Phase 2 scenarios ([[04 - Der Flüsternde Sender]], [[05 - Das sich windende Herz]], [[06 - Die Withering Zone]]), leading towards the eventual [[Checkpoint - Muster erkannt & Fokuspunkt lokalisiert]]. Use this as a guide for your Obsidian Canvas.

**Core Principle:** Players enter Phase 2 with leads from Act 1 pointing towards A04, A05, and/or A06. Within each scenario, they should find clues confirming its primary focus (Quelle, Manifestation-Raum, Manifestation-Verfall/Lösung) but *also* potentially find cross-linking clues pointing towards the *other* Phase 2 scenarios or reinforcing the overarching goals.

**Scenario 04: [[04 - Der Flüsternde Sender]] (Focus: Quelle)**
*   **Primary Clues Found:** Data about [[Ghroth]] (astronomical/energetic origin of signal), nature of memetic hazard.
*   **Potential Cross-Leads:**
    *   **-> A05 (Herz):** Signal analysis reveals geometric patterns or spatial coordinates correlating with the Labyrinth/Nexus location. Kult communication mentions "Orte der Macht".
    *   **-> A06 (Zone):** Signal analysis shows correlation with energy readings from Withering Zones. Kult communication mentions recruiting the "Apathischen" or exploiting "Verfall". Research notes mention failed attempts at shielding (-> Ziel 3).
    *   **-> Kult (Ziel 4):** Direct evidence of Kult control/plans for the sender. Mitgliederlisten, communication logs.
    *   **-> Lösung (Ziel 3):** Notes on failed shielding attempts.

**Scenario 05: [[05 - Das sich windende Herz]] (Focus: Manifestation - Raum)**
*   **Primary Clues Found:** Nature of spatial distortion, properties/location of [[Der Nexus]] (potential [[Der Fokuspunkt]]).
*   **Potential Cross-Leads:**
    *   **-> A04 (Sender):** Strange frequencies or energy readings within the Labyrinth/Nexus match the Sender's signal. Kult notes mention coordinating with the "Stimme des Sängers".
    *   **-> A06 (Zone):** The Labyrinth's energy feels similar to the life-draining effect of the Withering Zone. Notes found mention "Oasen" as stable anchors or failed attempts to stabilize areas.
    *   **-> Kult (Ziel 4):** Evidence of Kult rituals at the Nexus, their navigation methods, plans to harness the Nexus.
    *   **-> Lösung (Ziel 3):** Discovery of Widerstand symbols/notes in stable areas, knowledge from a [[Defektes Mythos-Wesen]] about stabilization or the Widerstand.

**Scenario 06: [[06 - Die Withering Zone]] (Focus: Manifestation - Verfall & Lösung)**
*   **Primary Clues Found:** Mechanism of life drain, nature of [[Quelle des Verfalls]], potential protection methods ("Oasen"), contact/traces of [[Der sterbende Widerstandskult]].
*   **Potential Cross-Leads:**
    *   **-> A04 (Sender):** Survivors report hearing the "Gesang" or specific memetic compulsions linked to the Sender. Kultists here might use tech derived from the Sender.
    *   **-> A05 (Herz):** The [[Quelle des Verfalls]] has similar energy signatures or geometric properties to [[Der Nexus]]. Widerstand notes mention other "Orte der Macht" including the Labyrinth.
    *   **-> Kult (Ziel 4):** Direct confrontation with [[Transzendenz-Sucher]] / Kultists exploiting the zone. Their notes might reveal broader plans.
    *   **-> Lösung (Ziel 3):** Direct contact/knowledge from Widerstand, understanding the "Oasen" provides clues for [[Das Abschirmungsritual]].

**Towards the Checkpoint:**
*   The Checkpoint ([[Checkpoint - Muster erkannt & Fokuspunkt lokalisiert]]) occurs *after* A07. However, the clues gathered in A04, A05, A06 are essential for it.
*   **Key Synthesis:** Players (likely aided by [[Dr. Aris Thorne]] or Widerstand knowledge gained *after* A07) need to combine:
    *   Signal Origin/Data (from A04)
    *   Nexus Location/Properties/Energy Signature (from A05)
    *   Verfall Source Signature/Protection Principles (from A06)
    *   Historical/Ritual Context (from A06/Widerstand)
*   This synthesis allows the definitive identification of [[Der Fokuspunkt]] and the confirmation of the need for [[Das Abschirmungsritual]] and [[Die Komponente]].

**Keeper Notes:**
*   Seed cross-leads subtly. Not every scenario needs to point to every other one.
*   Use [[Dr. Aris Thorne]] or discovered texts to help players connect disparate clues if they struggle before the Checkpoint.
*   The *primary* goal of Phase 2 is gathering enough diverse information so the Checkpoint revelation feels earned after the trauma of A07.
